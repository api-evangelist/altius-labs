# Altius Labs

> Altius Labs builds customized, high-performance blockchain infrastructure for high-frequency onchain markets and trading applications. Founded by former Hudson River Trading engineers, it offers a VM-agnostic modular execution stack (Altius L1 settlement plus a high-performance execution coprocessor) that decouples execution from consensus and storage for parallel processing — targeting 10,000+ TPS and sub-millisecond latency while remaining EVM-compatible. Backed by Founders Fund, Pantera Capital, and Archetype.
